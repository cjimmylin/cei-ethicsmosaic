**Track-exclusive discovery breakdown:**
- NAICS uniquely discovers industry-specific trade associations (NPMA, MCAA, NFDA, AHLA) and supply-chain certification bodies (FSC, RSPO, WRAP).

## Methodology Notes

- Organization matching uses normalized name keys (lowercased, parenthetical abbreviations removed, whitespace collapsed).
- An organization appearing in multiple NAICS subsectors within the same sector is counted once per sector.
- Cross-sector codes listed in file-level `cross_sector_codes` arrays are included in NAICS counts.
- SOC files used varying key names (`major_groups`, `soc_groups`, `soc_major_groups`); all three were checked.
- Manufacturing Parts 1 and 2 are combined as a single NAICS 31-33 sector.
- Document type classification follows the council's 10-type taxonomy with additional types discovered during research.
