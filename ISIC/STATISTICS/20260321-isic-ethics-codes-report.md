# ISIC-Based Professional Ethics Codes Research — Final Report

**Project:** EthicsMosaic — Cross-Industry Professional Ethics Synthesis
**Report Date:** 2026-03-21
**Classification:** ISIC Rev 4 (21 sections, 88 divisions)
**Methodology:** Expert council + 12-agent swarm (4 waves × 3 parallel agents)
**Output directory:** `EthicsMosaic/ISIC/`

---

## Executive Summary

This report presents the results of a systematic search for professional codes of ethics, codes of conduct, ethics guidelines, and related documents across all 21 sections and 88 divisions of the International Standard Industrial Classification (ISIC) Rev 4. The search covered professional associations, regulatory bodies, labor unions, industry trade groups, and international federations operating in the US, UK, Australia, Canada, and at the international/global level.

**Key findings:**
- **473 documents** found from **422 organizations**
- **76 of 88 divisions** (86%) have at least one entry
- **12 divisions** have zero standalone ethics codes (all are industrial sub-divisions)
- Highest-yield sections: **M** (Professional, 63), **Q** (Health, 59), **K** (Finance, 49)
- Lowest-yield sections: **T** (Households, 2), **D** (Energy, 4), **U** (Extraterritorial, 10)
- Most unexpected finding: **textiles/apparel** (C13-14) yielded 9 codes — matching financial services — due to post-Rana Plaza accountability pressure

## Methodology

### Classification Frame
ISIC Rev 4 used as the primary organizing frame. For each division, the dominant professions were identified and searched separately. This approach ensures exhaustive sector coverage while preserving the ability to build an ISCO (occupational) crosswalk in Phase 2.

### Inclusion Criteria
- Document issued by a professional association, regulatory body, labor union, industry trade group, or international federation
- Contains explicit ethical content (values, principles, obligations, prohibitions)
- Publicly accessible with a verifiable URL
- Document types: codes of ethics, codes of conduct, ethics guidelines, standards of practice, oaths/pledges, industry principles

### Exclusion Criteria
- Individual company codes of conduct
- Blog posts, opinion pieces, academic papers on professional ethics
- Pure technical/safety standards with no ethical content

### Search Strategy
1. Expert council deliberation (7 experts, written format) — see `council-deliberation.md`
2. 4 sequential waves of 3 parallel agents, organized by expected yield:
   - Wave 1 (high-yield): Sections M, Q, K
   - Wave 2 (medium-yield): Sections P+J, O+R, F+H
   - Wave 3 (industry): Sections A+B, C Part 1, C Part 2
   - Wave 4 (services): Sections G+I, D+E+L, N+S+T+U
3. Search tools: WebSearch per profession, IIT CSEP database (ethics.iit.edu/codes), organizational website visits, snowball from references in found codes
4. Geographic priority: US → UK → Australia → Canada → EU-level → International federations

## Recommendations for Phase 2

### Priority 1: Geographic Expansion
1. **EU professional bodies:** CCBE (European lawyers), EFPA (European psychology), FEANI (European engineers)
2. **South Africa:** HPCSA (health), ECSA (engineering), LSSA (law)
3. **India:** BCI, MCI, ICAI — large profession populations with English codes
4. **Japan:** JSME, JSCE, JMA — exist but in Japanese

### Priority 2: Embedded Codes
5. **AFL-CIO affiliated union constitutions** — ethics embedded in bylaws, not standalone
6. **Postal/courier:** Direct contact with UNI Post & Logistics global union federation

### Priority 3: Data Quality
7. **URL validation pass** — verify all 473 URLs resolve (grey literature is unstable)
8. **Edition years** — add `current_edition_year` field to distinguish original publication from current version

### Priority 4: Schema Enrichment
9. **Cross-listing field** — add `cross_listed_isic` array for codes that apply to multiple sections
10. **Enforcement body** — add `enforcement_body` for who actually enforces the code
