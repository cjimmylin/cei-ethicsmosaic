### Notes
- **Strongest enforcement entries**: UCMJ (military), ICC Code of Judicial Ethics, DoD Directive 5500.07, UN Staff Rules, US FAM — all carry binding/statutory force.
- **Richest international coverage**: Bangalore Principles (judiciary), IAP Standards (prosecutors), ICRC/IHL (military), INTERPOL Code — all international in scope.
- **Notable gap**: No standalone, publicly published ethics code for national intelligence agencies (CIA, NSA, MI6) — the intelligence community operates under statutory conduct rules rather than published professional codes of ethics.
- **Tax administration**: OECD FTA provides integrity frameworks but no single universal code of ethics for tax officials comparable to the legal professions.
