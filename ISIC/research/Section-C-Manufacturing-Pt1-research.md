## Division 12 — Manufacture of Tobacco Products

### 12.1 Intergovernmental / Treaty

| # | Organization | Document | Type | Scope | Country | URL | Enforcement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | WHO | Framework Convention on Tobacco Control (FCTC) (2003; in force 2005) | International treaty with ethical provisions | Tobacco control regulators; indirectly governs sector | International | https://fctc.who.int/ | Treaty-based; 182 parties; binding on signatories |

### 12.2 Research / Scientific Bodies

| # | Organization | Document | Type | Scope | Country | URL | Enforcement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | CORESTA (Cooperation Centre for Scientific Research Relative to Tobacco) | Best Practices and Guidelines (CROM, research standards) | Scientific research standards | Tobacco and nicotine-product researchers | International | https://www.coresta.org/groups/coresta-standards | Aspirational; ISO TC/126 liaison |

**Notes:**
- The Tobacco Institute (disbanded 1998) was a lobbying group and published no ethics code.
- Individual tobacco company codes (BAT, PMI, Altria) are corporate codes, not professional/associational ethics — excluded per scope.
- CORESTA does not publish a standalone "code of ethics" but does set research best practice guidelines. Notable gap: no professional body for tobacco technologists with a formal ethics code.
- WHO FCTC Article 17 provides ethics-adjacent labor transition provisions; Article 5.3 prohibits industry interference in public health policy — direct ethical substance.
