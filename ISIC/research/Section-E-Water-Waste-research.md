## Research Notes and Gaps

- **Water sector:** Strong ethics infrastructure. Three distinct tracks: (1) US state-level water works codes (AWWA affiliates), (2) UK chartered professional codes (IWater, CIWEM), (3) wastewater-specific codes (WEF, NOWRA).
- **Solid waste:** SWANA is the key body for ethics in solid waste management.
- **Environmental/remediation:** NAEP is the most comprehensive ethics code for all environmental professionals. SETAC serves the scientific/research community specifically. IAIA addresses the EIA subspecialty.
- **Public works cross-listing:** APWA (American Public Works Association) Standards of Professional Conduct applies to public works infrastructure professionals managing water, sewer, and waste systems — classified under this section as a cross-listing from public administration.
- **International gap:** No strong international ethics body equivalent to NAEP for non-UK countries (IWA lacks a formal code).
- **Appraisal Practice Standard USPAP:** Not applicable here (applies to real estate appraisers, Section L).
