**Notes on 35.3:**

- WNA Charter of Ethics binds member organizations to transparency, safety culture, preventing illicit nuclear material use, and IAEA Safety Standards compliance.
- IAEA guidance publication (Nuclear Series) provides a framework for establishing ethics codes in national nuclear industries.

## Research Notes and Gaps

- **Electric utility professionals:** No major US electric utility professional association has a distinct member code of ethics separate from engineering bodies (IEEE, NSPE).
- **Energy traders/brokers:** No NEMA, WMEA, or WPTF ethics code discovered.
- **Gas distribution:** AGA is a trade group; individual gas professionals rely on engineering codes (ASME, NSPE).
- **Nuclear:** Strongest ethics infrastructure of Section D, with ANS (individual), WNA (corporate), and IAEA (national/international guidance).
- **Key cross-listing:** ASHRAE (HVAC engineering) ethics applies to air conditioning supply professionals but is classified under Section M/F (engineering professional services).
