## Notes

- **ICMM Mining Principles** are the most important industry-level ethics framework globally; Principle 1 ("Ethical Business") is explicitly an ethics commitment. Membership mandatory compliance.
- **SME Code of Ethics** is the primary US professional body instrument for all mining and exploration engineers.
- **AusIMM** is the leading professional body for Australia/NZ; JORC and VALMIN technical codes add reporting ethics.
- **SPE Code** (12 Canons) is the most developed petroleum engineering professional ethics document; covers safety, environment, honesty, anti-corruption.
- **AAPG Code of Ethics** covers petroleum geologists specifically; distinct from SPE which covers engineers.
- **AIPG Code of Ethics** is notable for its three-tier structure: Canons (aspirational), Ethical Standards (goals), Rules of Conduct (mandatory).
- **GSA Code** (2019) was the first enforceable ethics code in GSA's history; 15 complaints received, 6 resulting in sanctions since adoption.
- **EITI Standard** is a transparency/accountability framework rather than a professional ethics code but carries significant normative ethical content around anti-corruption and revenue transparency.
- **MAC TSM** is the world's first mining standard requiring site-level reporting with external verification.
- **IFA Protect & Sustain**: Primarily a safety/sustainability certification, not a professional ethics code; included under Division 01 (Agriculture) rather than here.
- **IOGP**: No standalone code of ethics document found; IOGP focuses on best practices and safety guidance rather than member ethics codes.
- **PESGB**: No dedicated ethics code found in search results.
- **United Mine Workers of America (UMWA)**: No dedicated code of ethics document found.
