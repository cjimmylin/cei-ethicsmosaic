## Notes

- **SAF** (Division 02) is the most comprehensive forestry professional ethics document; adopted by member referendum and enforceable.
- **FAO CCRF** (Division 03) is the cornerstone international fisheries ethics instrument — voluntary but universally cited; underpins MSC and national fisheries law.
- **IFOAM Principles** (Division 01) are foundational for organic farming ethics worldwide; the four principles (Health, Ecology, Fairness, Care) are explicitly normative/ethical.
- **ILO C188** (Division 03) is the binding international labor standards instrument for fishing workers; contains extensive worker rights provisions with strong ethical content.
- **Boone & Crockett** and **Pope & Young** (Division 01.2) represent the most developed hunting ethics frameworks in North America; Fair Chase is the core concept.
- **FSC/PEFC** are multi-stakeholder certification systems with strong ethical content (worker rights, indigenous rights, environmental stewardship) but are sustainability standards rather than professional codes.
- **IUFRO**: No dedicated code of ethics found; expresses integrity/diversity values but no formal ethics document.
- **World Aquaculture Society**: No standalone code of ethics identified; ethics addressed only in publication guidelines.
- **Fisheries Society of British Isles (FSBI)**: No dedicated ethics or professional conduct document found.
- **National Farmers Union (US)**: Ethical values embedded in policy platform but no standalone code of ethics document.
