# Section N — Administrative and Support Service Activities
## Research Notes (ISIC Rev. 4, Divisions 77–82)

**Date:** 2026-03-21
**Researcher:** Claude Code (agent)
**Wave:** 4

---

## Overview

Section N spans a wide range of business support activities. Ethics coverage is uneven across divisions. Division 78 (Employment Activities) is the richest: HR, staffing, and executive search each have mature, enforced codes. Division 80 (Security and Investigation) is the second richest, with ASIS International and ACFE both running highly developed ethics programs. Division 81 (Facility/Landscape) is moderately covered (IFMA, ASLA). Division 82 (Office Administrative) has IAAP. Division 77 (Rental/Leasing) has ELFA and ARDA. Division 79 (Travel Agency) is covered under ASTA, ABTA, and SITE — though IATA is a cross-listing from H51.

## Division 77 — Rental and Leasing Activities

### ELFA — Equipment Leasing and Finance Association
- **Document:** Code of Fair Business Practices
- **URL:** https://www.elfaonline.org/about/code-of-fair-business-practices
- **Notes:** Establishes industry principles for ethical conduct and professional service. Membership-binding. Trade association code focused on equipment leasing/financing companies.
- **Scope:** US (international members)
- **Enforcement:** Self-regulatory / membership requirement

### ARDA — American Resort Development Association
- **Document:** ARDA Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://www.arda.org/about/code-ethics/
- **PDF:** https://www.arda.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/arda-code-of-ethics.pdf
- **Notes:** Covers vacation ownership (timeshare) industry. Very detailed: general conduct, consumer-facing disclosure standards, resort management, resale activities, enforcement procedures. Membership is conditional on compliance.
- **Scope:** US
- **Enforcement:** Binding membership requirement with enforcement committee

### BOMA — Building Owners and Managers Association
- **Document:** Code of Ethics (referenced in bylaws and local chapter pages)
- **URL:** https://www.boma.org/ (no standalone public-facing ethics page found at international level)
- **Notes:** BOMA local chapters (e.g., BOMA Phoenix, BOMA New York) publish the code; the international body references it via governance documents. Educational course on ethics exists: https://learn.boma.org/products/is-there-an-ethical-dilemma-in-commercial-real-estate. Direct public URL not located — code appears to be member-gated or embedded in bylaws PDF.
- **Status:** Confirmed existence, URL not publicly resolved
- **Scope:** US / International

### ACRA — American Car Rental Association
- **Document:** Code of Ethics (mentioned in membership descriptions)
- **URL:** https://www.acraorg.com/ (no dedicated ethics page found in search)
- **Notes:** The ACRA website references ethical standards for members but no dedicated publicly-accessible code of ethics page was found. Industry is lightly regulated by a trade association with ethical norms embedded in membership terms.
- **Status:** Gap — no publicly accessible code found

## Division 78 — Employment Activities

### SHRM — Society for Human Resource Management
- **Document:** SHRM Code of Ethical and Professional Standards in Human Resource Management
- **URL:** https://www.shrm.org/legal/bylaws-and-code-of-ethics/code-of-ethics
- **Notes:** One of the most influential HR ethics frameworks. First developed 1972. Covers 6 core principles: professional responsibility, professional development, ethical leadership, fairness and justice, conflicts of interest, use of information. Broad global reach.
- **Scope:** US (global influence)
- **Enforcement:** Aspirational + professional standards; SHRM-CP/SCP certification requires adherence

### CIPD — Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (UK)
- **Document:** Code of Conduct and Ethics (revised 2023)
- **URL:** https://www.cipd.org/en/membership/professional-standards/code-of-conduct/
- **PDF:** https://www.cipd.org/globalassets/media/comms/code-of-conduct/2023-cipd-code-of-conduct-and-ethics.pdf
- **Notes:** Royal Charter body; code is legally grounded. Applies to all chartered members (CIPD, MCIPD, FCIPD). Superseded prior Code of Professional Conduct effective Jan 2023. Disciplinary cases published online. Enforcement mechanism is active.
- **Scope:** UK (international members)
- **Enforcement:** Statutory-adjacent (Royal Charter); disciplinary cases published; sanctions possible

### HRPA — Human Resources Professionals Association (Canada)
- **Document:** Code of Ethics and Rules of Professional Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.hrpa.ca/resources/ (resources page; PDF at https://www.hrpa.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/HRPA-Code-of-Ethics-and-Rules-of-Professional-Conduct.pdf)
- **Notes:** HRPA is a self-regulatory body under Ontario law (Human Resources Professionals Act, 2013). Code is legally grounded. Updated 2022. Disciplinary process exists.
- **Scope:** Canada (Ontario-based)
- **Enforcement:** Statutory self-regulatory body; disciplinary process

### ASA — American Staffing Association
- **Document:** Code of Ethics and Good Practices (multiple codes)
- **URL:** https://americanstaffing.net/asa/asa-code-of-ethics/
- **Sub-codes:**
  - Search and Placement: https://americanstaffing.net/asa/asa-code-of-ethics/search-and-placement-code-of-ethics/
  - Day Labor: https://americanstaffing.net/asa/asa-code-of-ethics/code-of-ethics-day-labor/
- **Notes:** Covers staffing firms' relations with employees, clients, and competitors. Membership pledge required.
- **Scope:** US
- **Enforcement:** Self-regulatory / membership requirement

### NAPS — National Association of Personnel Services
- **Document:** Code of Ethics and Standards of Ethical Practices
- **URL:** https://www.naps360.org/page/CodeOfEthics (Code of Ethics)
- **Alt URL:** https://www.naps360.org/page/StandardsOfEthical (Standards of Ethical Practices)
- **Notes:** Applies to permanent placement/executive recruiting firms. Binding on accredited members. NAPS Accreditation (NAF) specifically requires Code of Ethics compliance.
- **Scope:** US
- **Enforcement:** Accreditation-linked enforcement

### AESC — Association of Executive Search and Leadership Consultants
- **Document:** Code of Professional Conduct and Professional Practice Standards
- **URL:** https://www.aesc.org/standards/
- **Alt:** https://www.aesc.org/about-us/our-standards
- **Notes:** Revised regularly. Values: ethics & integrity, excellence, objectivity, diversity & inclusion. Also includes Client Bill of Rights and Candidate Bill of Rights.
- **Scope:** International (US-based)
- **Enforcement:** Membership binding; complaint process exists

### WorldatWork
- **Document:** Code of Ethics (embedded in certification requirements)
- **URL:** https://worldatwork.org/certifications (certification handbook PDF at https://worldatwork.org/media/CDN/dist/CDN2/documents/pdf/Education/2023%20Certification%20Handbook%20v4.pdf)
- **Notes:** No standalone public ethics page found. WorldatWork certifications (CCP, CECP, CSCP) require commitment to ethical practice of compensation, benefits, and Total Rewards. Ethics is embedded in certification handbook rather than a standalone public document.
- **Scope:** US / International
- **Status:** Confirmed existence; no standalone public URL — embedded in certification framework

## Division 79 — Travel Agency, Tour Operators, Reservation Services

### ASTA — American Society of Travel Advisors
- **Document:** Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://www.asta.org/about/content.cfm?ItemNumber=745
- **Notes:** Core principles: accuracy, disclosure. All ASTA members pledge adherence. One of the oldest and most established travel industry ethics frameworks.
- **Scope:** US (international members)
- **Enforcement:** Self-regulatory; membership condition

### ABTA — Association of British Travel Agents
- **Document:** Code of Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.abta.com/about-us/code-of-conduct
- **PDF:** https://www.abta.com/sites/default/files/2020-10/Code%20of%20Conduct%20Oct%202020.pdf
- **Notes:** Over 60 years old; regularly updated. Covers advertising accuracy, fair terms of trading, booking changes, complaint management. Code of Conduct Committee enforces compliance. Often cited as a role model for other industries.
- **Scope:** UK
- **Enforcement:** ABTA Code of Conduct Committee; sanctions including membership suspension

### SITE — Society for Incentive Travel Excellence
- **Document:** Code of Ethics & Conduct
- **URL:** https://siteglobal.com/site-code-ethics-conduct/
- **Alt:** https://www.siteglobal.com/p/cm/ld/fid=25
- **Notes:** International; 3,600+ members in 90 countries. Members agree to embrace the SITE Creed and Code of Ethics. Complaint process outlined on website.
- **Scope:** International
- **Enforcement:** Self-regulatory; complaint mechanism

### USTOA — United States Tour Operators Association
- **Document:** Code of Ethics (embedded in membership requirements)
- **URL:** https://ustoa.com/about
- **Notes:** Strict code described on membership/about pages. Covers truth in advertising, accurate pricing, consumer protection. No dedicated standalone ethics page found; code is embedded in membership terms rather than a separate document.
- **Scope:** US
- **Status:** Confirmed existence via membership pages; no standalone URL

### IATA — International Air Transport Association [CROSS-LISTING from H51]
- **Notes:** IATA Code of Business Conduct found in H51 (Air Transport). Relevant here for travel agency accreditation and standards in Division 79. Cross-list only.
- **Original section:** H — Transportation and Storage, Division 51

## Division 80 — Security and Investigation Activities

### ASIS International (formerly American Society for Industrial Security)
- **Document:** Code of Ethics (and separate Code of Conduct)
- **URL:** https://www.asisonline.org/security-news/code-of-ethics-and-code-of-conduct/code-of-ethics/
- **Alt (hub):** https://www.asisonline.org/security-news/code-of-ethics-and-code-of-conduct/
- **Certification Code:** https://www.asisonline.org/certification/professional-code-of-conduct/
- **Notes:** The flagship global security management professional ethics document. Covers security professionals of all specialties. Separate certification-specific Code of Professional Responsibility for CPP, PSP, PCI holders.
- **Scope:** International (US-based)
- **Enforcement:** Membership and certification conditions; disciplinary process

### ACFE — Association of Certified Fraud Examiners
- **Document:** CFE Code of Professional Standards + Code of Professional Ethics
- **URL:** https://www.acfe.com/about-the-acfe/acfe-rules-of-conduct-and-disciplinary-procedures
- **Notes:** All CFE members must abide by the Code of Professional Ethics. Five principles: integrity, objectivity, confidentiality, competency, professionalism. Separate Rules of Conduct with disciplinary procedures. Very detailed enforcement.
- **Scope:** International (US-based)
- **Enforcement:** Strict; disciplinary procedures; credential revocation possible

### NCISS — National Council of Investigation & Security Services
- **Document:** Code of Ethics and Professional Standards
- **URL:** https://www.nciss.org/content.aspx?page_id=22&club_id=58484&module_id=495464
- **Alt (2021 version):** https://www.nciss.org/code-of-ethics-2021
- **Notes:** Covers private investigators and security services firms. Principles include competency, confidentiality, conflict of interest, truth in advertising. Binding on member firms.
- **Scope:** US
- **Enforcement:** Self-regulatory membership requirement

### NALI — National Association of Legal Investigators
- **Document:** Code of Ethics (adopts external professional standard)
- **URL:** https://nalionline.org/become-a-member/code-of-ethics/
- **Notes:** NALI Executive Council voted in 2023 to adopt "Code of Professional Conduct: Standards and Ethics for the Investigative Profession, 4th ed." by Kitty Hailey as its governing standard. Focuses on legal investigation specifically (attorneys' investigations).
- **Scope:** US
- **Enforcement:** Membership requirement; CLI credential linked

### NAPPS — National Association of Professional Process Servers
- **Document:** Membership Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://napps.org/Members_code.aspx
- **Notes:** All work must be performed in a lawful, professional, and ethical manner. Nothing may impugn the position, reputation, or name of the Association. Membership binding.
- **Scope:** US
- **Enforcement:** Membership requirement

### (ISC)² and ISACA [CROSS-LISTING from J62]
- **Notes:** (ISC)² Code of Ethics and ISACA Code of Professional Ethics were found in Section J (ICT). Both cybersecurity-focused but relevant for Div. 80 when security professionals are engaged in cybersecurity/investigation roles. Cross-list only.

## Division 81 — Services to Buildings and Landscape Activities

### IFMA — International Facility Management Association
- **Document:** Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://www.ifma.org/about/governance/code-of-ethics
- **Notes:** All IFMA members required to comply. Covers professional integrity, client service, conflict of interest. Strong institutional backing (CFM credential).
- **Scope:** International (US-based)
- **Enforcement:** Membership and certification requirement

### ASLA — American Society of Landscape Architects
- **Document:** Code of Professional Ethics
- **URL:** https://www.asla.org/about/ethics-and-professional-conduct/code-of-professional-ethics
- **Alt URL:** https://www.asla.org/ContentDetail.aspx?id=4276
- **Notes:** Long-established code. Licensed professional body. Also has a separate Code of Environmental Ethics (https://www.asla.org/about/ethics-and-professional-conduct/asla-code-of-environmental-ethics). Ethics History page traces evolution since early 20th century.
- **Scope:** US
- **Enforcement:** Professional licensure context; membership requirement

### IIDA — International Interior Design Association
- **Document:** Code of Ethics for Professional and Associate Member Conduct
- **URL:** https://iida.org/membership (membership page references code; direct ethics page not found publicly)
- **Alt URL (chapter):** https://www.iida-or.org/membership/iida-code-of-ethics
- **Notes:** Code is embedded in membership terms. Document itself available via Scribd (https://www.scribd.com/document/427260924/design-ethics) but no standalone IIDA.org public URL found. Discipline addressed in IIDA FAQ.
- **Scope:** International (US-based)
- **Status:** Confirmed code exists; no standalone public IIDA.org ethics URL

### ASID — American Society of Interior Designers [Note: Interior design overlaps Div. 81 building services]
- **Document:** Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.asid.org/resources/about/ethics
- **PDF direct:** https://www.asid.org/lib24watch/files/download/11528/72acfb02e2b83e8325fda91203f24b1c90213ebf
- **Notes:** All ASID members agree to abide by the code. Covers interior design practice broadly. ASID is more tightly coupled to architectural interiors than IIDA but significant overlap.
- **Scope:** US
- **Enforcement:** Membership requirement; NCIDQ exam linked

### BSCAI — Building Service Contractors Association International
- **Document:** Code of Ethics (referenced in RBSM designation brochure)
- **URL:** https://www.bscai.org/ (main site; no standalone public ethics page found)
- **Notes:** Code exists (referenced in BSCAI's Registered Building Service Manager [RBSM] brochure PDF https://www.bscai.org/Portals/0/RBSM%20Brochure.pdf) but not accessible as standalone public document.
- **Status:** Confirmed existence; no public standalone URL

## Assessment

Division 78 (Employment) is exceptionally rich — SHRM, CIPD, HRPA, ASA, NAPS, and AESC together represent a comprehensive coverage of HR and staffing ethics. Division 80 (Security/Investigation) is also well-covered. Division 81 (Facility/Landscape) has IFMA, ASLA, and ASID as clear entries. Division 79 has ASTA, ABTA, and SITE. Division 77 has ELFA and ARDA. Division 82 is weaker — IAAP is the primary standalone code.
