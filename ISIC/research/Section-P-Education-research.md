## Section P Summary: 18 entries from 18 organizations

### Coverage Notes
- Strong coverage of US professional associations across all sub-professions
- International (EI, IFLA, ARMA) and UK (NEU, CILIP) represented
- Notable gap: Australia (AEU — Australian Education Union) has no verified standalone code of ethics found; it operates through union agreements
- Notable gap: AAUP's 1940 Statement of Academic Freedom and Tenure is foundational but not strictly a code of ethics; the 2009 Statement on Professional Ethics is the most direct equivalent
- The MCEE (NASDTEC) is significant as a multi-organization consensus document co-developed with NEA, AFT, NASSP, NAESP, CCSSO, and AACTE
- NASP has stronger enforcement (NCSP certification revocation) than most education codes
