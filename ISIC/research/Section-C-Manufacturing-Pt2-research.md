## Gaps and Zero-Coverage Divisions

Divisions 22, 24, 27, 28, 31, and 33 have no standalone professional ethics codes identified. These sectors:
- Rely on general engineering professional codes (Section M)
- Use industry certification/management system standards (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001)
- Follow supply chain codes from upstream/downstream (RBA for electronics supply chains, Responsible Care for chemical inputs)
- Have sustainability charters (AISI for steel, Portland Cement Association) that do not rise to formal ethics codes

This pattern is consistent with lower professional guild density in these sectors compared to chemistry or pharmaceuticals.
