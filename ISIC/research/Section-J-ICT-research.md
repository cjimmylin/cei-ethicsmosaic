### Coverage Notes
- Division 62 (computing/cybersecurity) is the best-covered with 9 entries spanning computing, software engineering, and cybersecurity sub-professions
- Division 58 (journalism) is well-covered: SPJ (US), IFJ (global), NUJ (UK), ASNE/APME (US)
- Division 60 (broadcasting): BBC Editorial Guidelines and CBC JSP are quasi-regulatory instruments rather than professional association codes but are universally recognized
- Division 61 (telecom): only IEEE covers this space as a professional body; no telecom-specific professional code found
- Notable gaps: no ICCO global PR code verified; no standalone advertising ethics code from 4A's (American Association of Advertising Agencies) or IAA
- WGA and DGA codes are labor/creative-rights focused; ethical dimensions are present but not foregrounded in the same way as traditional codes of ethics
- SAA (archivists) appears under Section P (Division 85, libraries/archivists); it could also map to Division 63 (information services)
