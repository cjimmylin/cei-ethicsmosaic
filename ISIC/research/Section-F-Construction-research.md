# Section F — Construction

**ISIC Rev 4 Divisions 41–43**
**Researcher notes:** Construction has strong ethics codes at the trade-association level (AGC, ABC, NAHB, AIC) and for chartered professional bodies (RICS, CIOB, CMAA, PMI). Specialty trades have PHCC for plumbing/HVAC, ASHRAE for HVAC engineers, PCA for painters. No single regulatory board equivalent to an engineering licensing board covers all of construction; ethics is therefore fragmented across sub-profession associations. FIDIC covers consulting engineers who design and oversee construction but is shared with Section M.

---

## Division 41 — Construction of Buildings

### AGC of America — Code of Ethical Conduct
- **Organization:** Associated General Contractors of America (AGC)
- **Document:** Code of Ethical Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.agcwa.com/agcs-code-of-ethical-conduct/ (AGC of Washington chapter; national code)
- **Year:** December 2000 (AGC of Washington Board adoption)
- **Key principles:** Honesty ("My word is my bond"), safety above profit, environmental stewardship, quality delivery, accountability, dispute resolution through negotiation.
- **Core values:** Skill, Responsibility, Integrity.
- **Enforcement:** Voluntary; no formal sanctions specified.
- **Scope:** US (national; state chapters adopt).

### NAHB — Code of Ethics
- **Organization:** National Association of Home Builders (NAHB)
- **Document:** NAHB Code of Ethics / Member Code of Conduct
- **URL:** https://hbaconnect.nahb.org/codeofconduct
- **Year:** Not specified (current online version)
- **Key principles:** Highest standards of service in the building industry; not performing acts that bring the building industry into disrepute; support for free enterprise; commitment to customers, community, and country.
- **Enforcement:** Adherence required for membership; local HBAs enforce.
- **Scope:** US

### ABC — Code of Ethics
- **Organization:** Associated Builders and Contractors (ABC)
- **Document:** ABC Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://www.abc.org/Membership/Code-of-Ethics
- **Year:** Adopted 1971; reaffirmed 2006, 2022
- **Key principles:** Performance meeting owner expectations; honest pricing and realistic timelines; professional collaboration with architects/engineers; prompt payment; highest safety standards; fair compensation; workforce development.
- **Enforcement:** Referenced in ABC Bylaws Articles I and III; Accredited Quality Contractors (AQC) recognition for sustained compliance.
- **Scope:** US

### AIC — Code of Ethics (American Institute of Constructors)
- **Organization:** American Institute of Constructors (AIC)
- **Document:** AIC Constructor Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://aic-builds.org/ethics/code-of-ethics/
- **Year:** Last modified November 2025
- **Key principles:** Full regard for public interest; no deceptive practices; no malicious injury to professional reputation; unbiased advice; continuous learning; support for research and education; professional behavior in all dealings.
- **Applies to:** All AIC Members, Certified Associate Constructors (CACs), and Certified Professional Constructors (CPCs).
- **Enforcement:** Professional certification renewal requires ethical conduct affirmation.
- **Scope:** US (international reach via certifications)

### CMAA — Code of Professional Conduct
- **Organization:** Construction Management Association of America (CMAA)
- **Document:** Code of Professional Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.cmaanet.org/about-us/code-professional-conduct
- **Year:** Effective requirements updated 2021
- **Key principles:** Four foundational principles — Ethical Practice, Professional Excellence, Responsibility to the Public, Program/Project-Centered Practice. Zero-tolerance harassment. Non-discrimination. Environmental sustainability guidance. Conflict-of-interest avoidance.
- **Enforcement:** CCMs, CACMs, and CMITs must affirm adherence at certification renewal; one PDH in ethics/safety required for renewal since 2021.
- **Scope:** US (international certifications)

## Division 42 — Civil Engineering

*Note: Civil engineering contractors are primarily covered by engineering professional bodies (ASCE, ICE) under Section M — Professional, Scientific & Technical Activities. FIDIC also applies to consulting engineers overseeing civil construction.*

### FIDIC — Code of Ethics
- **Organization:** International Federation of Consulting Engineers (FIDIC)
- **Document:** FIDIC Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://fidic.org/fidic-code-ethics; PDF: https://fidic.org/sites/default/files/fidic_codeofethics_201510.pdf
- **Year:** October 2015 edition
- **Key principles:** Responsibility to society; sustainable development; knowledge maintenance; due skill and care; Quality-Based Selection (QBS); no injury to others' reputation; no fee-based selection influence; cooperation with investigative bodies; no conflicting employment relationships.
- **Enforcement:** Member association-level; voluntary professional commitment.
- **Scope:** International

### RICS — Rules of Conduct
- **Organization:** Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS)
- **Document:** RICS Rules of Conduct (Global, October 2021; effective February 2022)
- **URL:** https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/rics-standards-and-guidance/conduct-competence/rules-of-conduct; PDF: https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/standards/2021_roc_en.pdf
- **Year:** 2022 (supersedes 2020 version)
- **Key principles:** Five rules based on ethical principles of honesty, integrity, competence, service, respect, and responsibility. Sustainability, data use, and diversity/inclusion incorporated in 2021 update.
- **Enforcement:** Mandatory; serious breaches lead to RICS disciplinary action. Members re-affirm annually.
- **Applies to:** All RICS members, candidates, students, and regulated firms. Covers quantity surveyors, building surveyors, and other construction-related surveyors.
- **Scope:** International (UK/Global)

### AIQS — Code of Professional Conduct
- **Organization:** Australian Institute of Quantity Surveyors (AIQS)
- **Document:** AIQS Code of Professional Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.aiqs.com.au/standards; direct PDF: https://www.quantitysurveyors.com.au/images/docs/Code%20of%20Conduct%20-aiqs.com.au_documents_Code%20of%20Conduct%202014.pdf
- **Year:** 2014 (current edition)
- **Key principles:** Integrity, professionalism, transparency in dealings; CPD requirements; complaint handling procedures; ethics definition and application.
- **Enforcement:** Binding on all members; formal complaint-handling procedure.
- **Scope:** Australia (international membership)

### PMI — Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct
- **Organization:** Project Management Institute (PMI)
- **Document:** PMI Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.pmi.org/about/ethics; PDF: https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/ethics/pmi-code-of-ethics.pdf
- **Year:** Effective November 17, 2025 (updated version)
- **Key principles:** Four core values — Honesty, Responsibility, Respect, and Fairness. Aspirational and mandatory standards for each. Applies to PMP-certified project managers including in construction.
- **Enforcement:** Required for PMP/CAPM certification holders; ethics complaint process managed by PMI.
- **Scope:** International

## Division 43 — Specialized Construction Activities

### PHCC — Code of Ethics (Plumbing-Heating-Cooling)
- **Organization:** Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors National Association (PHCC)
- **Document:** PHCC Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://www.phccweb.org/about/code-of-ethics/
- **Year:** June 22, 2018 (last modified June 25, 2019)
- **Key principles:** Ten commitments including professional pride; continuous improvement; ethical standards above business success; honest advertising, pricing, and installation; regulatory compliance with all building/safety codes; water and energy conservation promotion.
- **Enforcement:** Membership requirement; local chapter enforcement.
- **Scope:** US

### ASHRAE — Code of Ethics (HVAC Engineers)
- **Organization:** American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE)
- **Document:** ASHRAE Code of Ethics (Section 1.140.001.1)
- **URL:** https://www.ashrae.org/about/governance/code-of-ethics
- **Year:** Adopted 1974; revised 1986, 2007, 2013
- **Key principles:** Public welfare; resource stewardship (energy, natural, human, financial); competence limits; professional care; conflict disclosure; confidentiality; alignment with own professional associations' ethics codes; cultural respect.
- **Enforcement:** Applies to conduct within ASHRAE activities; formal Ethics Complaint Form process.
- **Scope:** International

### ACCA — Code of Conduct (HVAC Contractors)
- **Organization:** Air Conditioning Contractors of America (ACCA)
- **Document:** ACCA Code of Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.acca.org/codeofconduct
- **Year:** Current (© 2026 footer)
- **Key principles:** Civil and professional dialogue; no commercial spam or defamatory content; copyright respect; antitrust compliance (no price/margin discussions); respectful community engagement.
- **Enforcement:** ACCA reserves right to reject messages or ban users; decisions final.
- **Notes:** This is primarily a community platform code rather than a full professional ethics code. Substantive industry ethics come from state licensing boards.
- **Scope:** US

### PCA — Code of Ethics (Painters)
- **Organization:** Painting Contractors Association (PCA; formerly PDCA)
- **Document:** PCA Code of Ethics
- **URL:** https://www.pcapainted.org/about/code-of-ethics/
- **Year:** Current (PDF standards May 2019)
- **Key principles:** Professional service delivery; protecting and preserving property; regulatory/insurance/licensing compliance; faithful contract fulfillment; continuous knowledge expansion; exemplary conduct for peers to emulate; use of industry-standard materials and techniques.
- **Enforcement:** Required for membership; member must be licensed/registered.
- **Scope:** US

### CIOB — Rules and Regulations of Professional Competence and Conduct (UK)
- **Organization:** Chartered Institute of Building (CIOB)
- **Document:** Rules and Regulations of Professional Competence and Conduct
- **URL:** https://www.ciob.org/ethics-respect-people; direct PDF: https://www.ciob.org/media/498/download
- **Year:** Effective January 1, 2018 (relaunched after review)
- **Key principles:** Public interest paramount; competence consistent with membership class; integrity at all times; upholding dignity and reputation of the Institute. Ethical standards page also addresses modern slavery, corruption, and crime in construction.
- **Enforcement:** Disciplinary procedures; Institute may require member to cease duties pending investigation.
- **Scope:** UK/International (CIOB has global membership)

## Coverage Assessment

**Well-covered professions:**
- General contractors (AGC, ABC)
- Home builders (NAHB)
- Construction managers (CMAA, AIC, CIOB)
- Quantity surveyors (RICS, AIQS)
- Project managers (PMI)
- Plumbing/HVAC contractors (PHCC, ASHRAE, ACCA)
- Painters (PCA)
- Consulting engineers overseeing construction (FIDIC)

**Gaps / Not found:**
- Dedicated electricians' professional code (NECA has event code of conduct only; IEC/IBEW no standalone ethics code found — refer to IBEW Constitution)
- Carpenters (UBC Constitution has member obligations but no dedicated ethics code; UBC in Alberta has conduct code)
- Demolition contractors: no dedicated professional ethics code found
- Roofers: no national association ethics code found
- Scaffolding/structural steel: no dedicated code found
- Quantity surveyors — US: RICS covers US members; no distinct US quantity surveying ethics body found
